The easy and recovery runs, read properly

The score charges every second above the ceiling identically. That is not how these runs are judged, and two new reported, never scored measurements are on every continuous run from this week forward so the difference is visible:

  • the excursion shape — how many times the ceiling was crossed, the longest single crossing, and how much of the total sat in stretches over two minutes;
  • r(pace, HR) — how much heart rate answered the pace within the run. Strongly negative is normal: slow down, it follows. Near zero means it did not.
