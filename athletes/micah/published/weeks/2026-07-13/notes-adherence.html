5000m pace this week is 5:58/mi, which puts a 1000 m rep at 3:42.5 with a ±5 s tolerance — ±1 s per 200 m, scaled. The band was [217.451, 227.451], and a 3:37 rep is 217 seconds. It forfeited the whole rep on 0.45 of a second.

Two things were wrong with that, and only one of them was arithmetic:

  • Split times are whole seconds. A band edge carrying three decimals is finer than the measurement it judges. Edges round outward now — floor the fast end, ceil the slow end — so the band is 3:37–3:48 and all three reps earn. Outward can only widen, so it can never invent a failure.
  • The failure text printed the edge as 217.451, in raw seconds to three decimals, beside a rep printed as 3:37. Read straight, that says the target is 3:37.45 and the tolerance is zero, which is what it looked like from the outside — the ±5 s was being applied and nothing on the page said so. Edges print as clocks now: 3:37.5.

All at target, with no relaxed finish. Repetition work used to relax its closing reps and interval work did not; the relaxation is deleted outright as of 2026-08-09 and both modes are graded by one rule. What still separates them is the default — an interval set names its own race pace or scores nothing, where repetition resolves the 800m-to-3000m zone.
