Runalyze's own reading of this week: A:C 1.38, above its optimum ceiling

It is the highest running-only ratio anywhere in the five-week record — 07-13 reads 1.13, 07-27 0.89 and 08-03 1.15 — and this note said nothing about it until 2026-08-09, because the numbers did not exist here.

They exist now because the athlete read CTL and ATL off Runalyze's form curve for 07-12 and supplied them. get_calculations takes no date argument, so that is the only route to a past week, and the two readings are all it takes: TSB is CTL − ATL and the A:C ratio is ATL / CTL, both verified against the one verbatim payload this repo holds. They live in snapshots/runalyze/2026-07-12.json under calculations_read_off_graph — a separate key from calculations, so verbatim keeps meaning verbatim, and every figure derived from them prints with a read off the curve label.

Rest days, monotony and strain are not plotted over time anywhere in Runalyze, so they stay unavailable. None of them is scored here; the week is whole without them.

Read this beside the adherence grade. Two independent models, looking at different data, both saying the same thing about the same week.

Three days were unpriced. Two were my shape, one is genuinely missing data

They were reported and left out of both sides of the ratio, never zeroed. Two are closed as of 2026-08-09, and the distinction between them matters: neither was ever a gap in what the athlete knew — the load skill simply had no field that could say it.

  • Tuesday, the mixed session — 400m, 600m, 400m, 200m at Repetition, 1 mile Sub-T. Four reps of four different lengths priced against a RACE pace, then one rep against a vVO2max band. The run-level rep_distance_m describes one uniform block and cannot express either half. It is priced per set now, with rep_distance_m allowed to be a list and rep_pace naming a race pace where rep_band names a band. The 200 m jog recovery is the library standard, the same authorship 2026-07-14's float_distance_m: 200 already has. 72 prescribed minutes, and it scores 100.
  • Saturday, one prescription in two Garmin files — 978 s and 1194 s against a single 30 min easy. Putting 1800 on either scores a 36-minute day as ten minutes short, so neither carries it, and then nothing priced the DATE. day_prescribed_seconds states what the plan priced the date at, which is the level the fact lives at, and overrides the per-run sum. It scores 82 — 20,026 SE against a 16,486 ceiling, and the overage is 4,461 SE of background on a 30-minute easy day, the largest background of the week.
  • Friday's 11x~.22 mile hill repeats is the one real gap, and it needs two numbers: how long one repeat takes and how long the recovery down is. Nothing can derive them — the pace chart has no band for uphill running, race_paces has no entry for a fifth of a mile uphill, and the library's only hill entry is 4-8x6-8s hill sprints, a different session. Taking them from what was run is refused on principle: a ceiling drawn from execution rises to meet the day.
