The hill sprints — reported, scored by nothing, and that is deliberate

3–5×6s hill sprints w/ 2–3 min walking recovery, 8:23 of file for 22 s of running. The model's target is 90–95% of maximal speed, which is athlete-supplied and athlete-agnostic; there is no criterion because the athlete intends to grade these on cadence and cannot say how until there is data.

This week does not supply that data either. Rep 1 (8 s) reads 224 spm off two non-zero samples; reps 2 and 3 (7 s each) have none at all. Garmin publishes running cadence on a rolling window, so a six-second rep yields a number about one time in three on this watch. Widening the window would print the walk back down the hill as a sprint cadence.

The walking recovery is priced at zero in both skills, so this file contributes 22 s and 0.086 km to the week rather than its 8:23 of wall clock.
