Data provenance

All seven days precede the 2026-08-03 Health Connect cutover, so all seven come from Garmin daily totals, which are authoritative for that period. Health Sync serves as a cross-check and agrees exactly on the five dates where its export carries watch-sourced rows (7/27, 7/28, 7/29, 7/30 and 8/03 — see notes/skill-review.md L1). On 7/31, 8/1 and 8/2 the export is phone-only, because those dates were snapshotted before Garmin backfilled into Health Connect; it undercounts them by 94%, 25% and 93% and is correctly ignored.

Run/background splits: 7/27–7/30 use step-stream-window, summing watch records inside each run's clock window — the only source drawn from the same records as the total. 7/31, 8/1 and 8/2 use cadence-stream, each run's own measured cadence from its Runalyze payload.

No day of this week is an estimate any more. Those three used to read cadence-estimate — 172 spm × duration, a population constant — because parse_steps.py did not default --payloads and never opened the cadence streams already sitting in raw/activities/. Across the whole series that made 45 of 57 days estimates, and since run_se = run_steps × 2.5, roughly 90% of a graded week's load was a product of that one constant with the background figure left as its residual. Switching to measurement moved this week's total by 0.4% and changed no conclusion, which is the result worth having: the findings did not depend on the assumption.
